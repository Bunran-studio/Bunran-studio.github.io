Privacy Policy

App: Jump Taro / Developer: Bunran / Effective date: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Bunran ("we") handles information in the mobile game "Jump Taro" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The App has no accounts.

Your game progress (cleared stages, best times, collected items, and settings such as volume and language) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers. Deleting the App also deletes this data.

3. Personalized ads and how to turn them off

The App may show ads tailored to your interests (personalized ads). Where the law requires consent, the App asks for it when you first launch it.

To turn off personalized ads, delete or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (for example "Settings → Google → Ads"; the exact menu depends on your device).

4. In-app purchases

You can buy the "Ad-Free Pack", which removes ads and includes an exclusive bonus character. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your credit card number or other payment details. Whether you have purchased the pack is checked between your device and Google Play.

5. Children

The App is mainly intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents, please supervise your children's use of the App.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not share it with third parties. As described in section 2, Google may collect information to deliver ads.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when necessary. Changes will be posted on this page.
